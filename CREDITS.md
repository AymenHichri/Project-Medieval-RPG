# Credits

_Kept in sync with `docs/asset_manifest.csv`. The in-game credits screen is generated from the manifest in Phase 12._

## Art
- **Characters, enemies, boss, environments, weather effects:** SovDigital, https://sovdigital.itch.io
  (Knight, Wolf, Skeleton, Goblin Scout, Blade Assassin, Medieval Village, Castle Courtyard, Necropolis, Weather Effects).
- **Ambience and weather scripts/shaders:** SovDigital (MIT-0).
- **Battle and map effects:** SovDigital (Free Spell Effects, Free Spell Patterns, Free Projectile Effects,
  Free Blood Splash, Free Ambient Effects).
- **HD item icons and equipment-slot silhouettes:** SovDigital (Free Inventory UI & Rarity Kit; only those parts are used).
- **UI kit, item and status icons, portraits, battle backgrounds:** "Art by Gimble's Games", https://gimblesgames.itch.io
  (Dark Fantasy Kit Sampler). Note: the vendor states these were made with AI-assisted generation.
- **Additional UI elements (dfgui kit):** source and licence **to be confirmed** (release blocker).
- **Placeholders in the vertical slice:** the Elder NPC uses the SovDigital Knight `black` recolour and
  Gimble's Witch Hunter portrait; the village well is the SovDigital Castle Courtyard well.
